In short

What is Goldenberry?

Goldenberry (Physalis peruviana) is a plant of the Solanaceae (nightshade family) family. Traditions that use it: Andes. Part used: fruit.

What is Goldenberry used for?

Traditional use: The orange fruit inside its papery husk is eaten fresh; in folk belief it is good for the eyes and throat. Scientific evidence: traditional. Contains carotenoids and vitamin C.

Is Goldenberry safe?

Safety label: caution. The ripe fruit is safe. Unripe fruit, leaves and husk contain poisonous solanine.

Traditional use

Andes

The orange fruit inside its papery husk is eaten fresh; in folk belief it is good for the eyes and throat.

Türkiye

In recent years it has been grown and sold in Türkiye as “altın çilek” (golden berry).

In nature

Where it grows
The valleys of the Andes between 1,500 and 3,000 metres; today it is grown in many countries.
Harvest season
Picked when the husk dries; all year in the Andes.
Local names
altın çilek, Peru çileği; local names aguaymanto, uchuva

Mix-up risk

Bladder cherry and other Physalis species look similar; do not eat unripe fruit.

Preparation

The fruit is eaten fresh or dried.

What science says

Contains carotenoids and vitamin C. There are no human studies of medicinal claims.

Safety

Caution

The ripe fruit is safe. Unripe fruit, leaves and husk contain poisonous solanine.

Drug interactions

Medicine or substanceWhy it matters
