In short

What is Ayahuasca vine?

Ayahuasca vine (Banisteriopsis caapi) is a plant of the Malpighiaceae family. Traditions that use it: Amazon. Part used: stem (vine).

What is Ayahuasca vine used for?

Traditional use: The core ingredient of the ayahuasca brew drunk with a healer (curandero) in ceremonies of the Shipibo, Asháninka and many other Amazonian peoples. Scientific evidence: limited. Contains harmine, harmaline and tetrahydroharmine, which inhibit MAO-A.

Is Ayahuasca vine safe?

Safety label: high risk. Can interact fatally with antidepressants and some migraine and heart medicines. In people with a history of psychosis it can trigger severe psychiatric episodes. Deaths and abuse have been reported at unregulated retreats. Legal status: Illegal in Türkiye.

Traditional use

Amazon

The core ingredient of the ayahuasca brew drunk with a healer (curandero) in ceremonies of the Shipibo, Asháninka and many other Amazonian peoples. It is used for healing, divination and contact with spirits; the icaros the healer sings shape the ceremony.

Peru today

In 2008 Peru declared the traditional ayahuasca practices of Amazonian communities national cultural heritage. The areas around Iquitos and Pucallpa are hubs of international ayahuasca tourism.

In nature

Where it grows
Humid forests of the Amazon basin (Peru, Ecuador, Colombia, Brazil). Today most is grown in gardens.
Harvest season
The vine is cut all year; thick, mature stems are preferred.
Local names
No Turkish folk name. Local names: ayahuasca, yagé, caapi, natem

Mix-up risk

Ayahuasca products on sale vary; synthetic MAO inhibitors or other plants may have been added.

Preparation

The vine is pounded and usually boiled for hours with chacruna leaves.

What science says

Contains harmine, harmaline and tetrahydroharmine, which inhibit MAO-A. This lets the DMT in chacruna work when swallowed. Small clinical studies on depression are promising but early.

Safety

High risk

Can interact fatally with antidepressants and some migraine and heart medicines. In people with a history of psychosis it can trigger severe psychiatric episodes. Deaths and abuse have been reported at unregulated retreats.

Drug interactions

Medicine or substanceWhy it matters
Antidepressants (SSRIs, SNRIs, MAO inhibitors, tricyclics)Serotonin syndrome: can be fatal. The risk lasts for weeks after stopping these drugs.
Triptans, tramadol, dextromethorphan, lithiumSerious interaction through serotonin.
Stimulants, MDMA, cocaine, ephedrineDangerous rise in blood pressure and heart rate.
Tyramine-rich foodsHypertensive crisis because of MAO inhibition.

This list is not complete. If you take regular medication, ask your pharmacist or doctor before trying Ayahuasca vine.

Legal status

Illegal in Türkiye

A DMT-containing brew counts as a narcotic.

Laws differ by country; check local rules before travelling with or buying any plant product.

Conservation

Note
Tourist demand puts pressure on wild vines; many communities are switching to cultivation.
