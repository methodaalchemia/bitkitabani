In short

What is Opium poppy?

Opium poppy (Papaver somniferum) is a plant of the Papaveraceae (poppy family) family. Traditions that use it: Anatolia and the Mediterranean. Part used: seeds, capsules.

What is Opium poppy used for?

Traditional use: Grown around Afyonkarahisar for thousands of years. Scientific evidence: clinical. The capsule latex contains morphine and codeine.

Is Opium poppy safe?

Safety label: high risk. Capsules and latex are addictive and can stop breathing. Using the plant as a home remedy is deadly, especially for children. Legal status: Licensed in Türkiye.

Traditional use

Anatolia

Grown around Afyonkarahisar for thousands of years. The seeds go into poppy-seed buns and poppy paste. Capsules used to be boiled to put crying babies to sleep; this practice is deadly.

Ancient Greece

Hypnos, god of sleep, and Thanatos, god of death, were depicted with poppy capsules.

In nature

Where it grows
Provinces where growing is permitted, such as Afyonkarahisar, Uşak, Burdur, Denizli and Kütahya.
Harvest season
The capsules ripen in June–July; the harvest is carried out only by licensed growers.
Local names
haşhaş, afyon, ak haşhaş, mavi haşhaş

Mix-up risk

The red corn poppy (Papaver rhoeas) is a wild relative with no opium alkaloids. Opium poppy leaves are blue-green and clasp the stem.

Preparation

Only the seeds and seed oil are used as food.

What science says

The capsule latex contains morphine and codeine. Many modern painkillers still come from the poppy; Türkiye is one of the few countries licensed to grow it as a raw material.

Safety

High risk

Capsules and latex are addictive and can stop breathing. Using the plant as a home remedy is deadly, especially for children.

Drug interactions

Medicine or substanceWhy it matters
Opioid painkillers, sedatives, sleeping pills, alcoholThe risk of stopped breathing and loss of consciousness multiplies; can be fatal.
Poppy seeds and drug testsSeed-heavy foods can make urine tests positive for opiates.

This list is not complete. If you take regular medication, ask your pharmacist or doctor before trying Opium poppy.

Legal status

Licensed in Türkiye

Growing is allowed only with a TMO licence in permitted provinces. Seeds and food products are unrestricted.

Laws differ by country; check local rules before travelling with or buying any plant product.
