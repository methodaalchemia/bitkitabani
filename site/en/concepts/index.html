A concept and a plant every day

Concepts

From shamanism to folk belief, from ritual to ancestral knowledge: a short, sourced concept every day, each paired with a plant.

  • AnimismBelief in innumerable spiritual beings concerned with human affairs, dwelling in the things of nature. · Plant: Ayahuasca vine
  • AyurvedaIndia’s traditional system of medicine: three bodily humours, five elements and a great many plants. · Plant: Ashwagandha
  • Dieta (sama)The regimen in the Peruvian Amazon in which a plant remedy is taken without salt, nearly fasting and in seclusion. · Plant: Chiric sanango
  • Doctrine of signaturesThe old belief that a plant’s shape or colour shows which ailment it treats. · Plant: Mandrake
  • Haoma and somaThe sacred plant and drink of Zoroastrianism and the soma of the Vedas: a plant whose identity is still debated. · Plant: Ephedra
  • Henna night (kına gecesi)The night, a few days before a wedding, when henna is applied to the bride’s hands; the best-known henna custom in Turkish culture. · Plant: Henna
  • IcaroThe healing songs that healers in the Peruvian Amazon believe they learn from plant spirits. · Plant: Mapacho
  • KamThe Altai and Tatar Turkic word for the shaman: an intermediary who travels to the sky and the underworld. · Plant: Juniper
  • LimpiaA cleansing ritual of Latin American folk healing, using plants, an egg and smoke. · Plant: Rue
  • MesaThe ritual altar of northern Peruvian folk healing, around which San Pedro ceremonies are held. · Plant: San Pedro cactus
  • Native American ChurchA North American religious movement that holds peyote sacred and combines Indigenous and Christian beliefs. · Plant: Peyote
  • Nazar (the evil eye)The belief that an envious or admiring gaze can cause harm; in Anatolia its best-known protector is Syrian rue. · Plant: Syrian rue
  • PachamamaMother Earth of the Andes, “fed” with offerings in August. · Plant: Coca
  • Prophetic medicine (tıbb-ı nebevî)“The Prophet’s medicine”: the Prophet Muhammad’s sayings on health and healing, and the books that grew from them. · Plant: Black seed
  • SmudgingBurning bundles of dried plants to cleanse with their smoke; a practice that passed from North American Indigenous ceremonies to the New Age movement. · Plant: White sage
  • Susto and mal aireIn Andean and Amazonian folk medicine: the soul fleeing the body after a fright, the blow of bad air, and illness caused by sorcery. · Plant: Mapacho
  • Teacher plantsPlants “with a mother” that Peruvian Amazonian healers believe give knowledge to their apprentices. · Plant: Chiric sanango
  • Traditional Chinese medicineA system of medicine at least 23 centuries old that seeks to prevent illness by keeping yin and yang in balance. · Plant: Ginseng
  • VegetalismoThe tradition of mestizo healers in the Peruvian Amazon who are believed to learn from plants. · Plant: Chiric sanango

About this site

A compilation made for knowledge and curiosity; it is not medical advice. Before using any plant, especially if you take medicines, are pregnant or breastfeeding, talk to a doctor or pharmacist.

Evidence level

Traditional: no or very few human studies
