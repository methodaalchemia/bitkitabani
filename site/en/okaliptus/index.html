In short

What is Eucalyptus?

Eucalyptus (Eucalyptus globulus) is a plant of the Myrtaceae family. Traditions that use it: Anatolia and the Mediterranean. Part used: leaves, essential oil.

What is Eucalyptus used for?

Traditional use: In the early Republic it was planted to drain the swamps of Çukurova and Tarsus and fight malaria; people call it the “malaria tree”. Scientific evidence: limited. The EMA recognises eucalyptus oil for relieving coughs in colds as a traditional use.

Is Eucalyptus safe?

Safety label: caution. Swallowing even a few millilitres of the oil can cause seizures and loss of consciousness in children. Do not apply it to the faces or noses of babies and young children. The steam can trigger attacks in people with asthma.

Traditional use

Anatolia

In the early Republic it was planted to drain the swamps of Çukurova and Tarsus and fight malaria; people call it the “malaria tree”. The leaves are boiled for colds and the steam inhaled.

Australia

Aboriginal peoples use the leaves for fever and wounds and cleanse places with smoke from the branches.

In nature

Where it grows
Planted trees in Çukurova and on the Mediterranean and Aegean coasts; not native.
Harvest season
Leaves can be picked all year.
Local names
okaliptüs, sıtma ağacı, ökaliptüs

Mix-up risk

Young and old leaves look very different: young ones are round and grey-blue, old ones long and sickle-shaped.

Preparation

Fresh or dried leaves are put in hot water and the steam inhaled; a few drops of oil are used for steam.

What science says

The EMA recognises eucalyptus oil for relieving coughs in colds as a traditional use. There are controlled studies in bronchitis and sinusitis with its main compound, cineole (eucalyptol).

Safety

Caution

Swallowing even a few millilitres of the oil can cause seizures and loss of consciousness in children. Do not apply it to the faces or noses of babies and young children. The steam can trigger attacks in people with asthma.

Drug interactions

Drugs broken down in the liverCineole speeds up liver enzymes and may lower some drug levels.
