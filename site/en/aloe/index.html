What is Aloe vera?

Aloe vera (Aloe vera) is a plant of the Asphodelaceae family. Traditions that use it: Anatolia and the Mediterranean. Part used: leaf gel, leaf latex.

What is Aloe vera used for?

Traditional use: In Ottoman pharmacy the dried leaf latex, called sarısabır, was used as a laxative. Scientific evidence: limited. Small studies suggest the gel may speed healing of mild burns and sunburn.

Is Aloe vera safe?

Safety label: caution. The gel is generally safe on the skin. The latex causes diarrhoea, cramps and potassium loss; drinking it is not recommended. Not for oral use in pregnancy.

Traditional use

Anatolia

In Ottoman pharmacy the dried leaf latex, called sarısabır, was used as a laxative. Today the leaf gel is applied to burns and sunburn; it is grown in pots in homes on the Mediterranean coast.

Arabian Peninsula and Africa

The island of Socotra was known for thousands of years as the source of the finest aloe.

In nature

Where it grows
Grown outdoors on Türkiye’s Mediterranean coast and in pots elsewhere.
Harvest season
Mature leaves can be cut all year.
Local names
aloe, sarısabır, sabır otu

Mix-up risk

Other Aloe and Agave species grown as ornamentals look similar; take gel only from Aloe vera.

Preparation

The clear gel inside the leaf is scooped out and applied to the skin. The yellow latex must be separated from the gel.

What science says

Small studies suggest the gel may speed healing of mild burns and sunburn. Leaf latex taken by mouth is a strong laxative; it has not been used in US over-the-counter laxatives since 2002. Leaf extract caused cancer in animal studies.

Safety

Caution

The gel is generally safe on the skin. The latex causes diarrhoea, cramps and potassium loss; drinking it is not recommended. Not for oral use in pregnancy.

Drug interactions

Medicine or substanceWhy it matters
Diuretics, corticosteroids, licoriceLeaf latex increases potassium loss.
Digoxin and heart rhythm drugsLow potassium raises the risk of toxicity.
Diabetes medicinesOral aloe may lower blood sugar.

This list is not complete. If you take regular medication, ask your pharmacist or doctor before trying Aloe vera.
