Linden or chamomile?

Linden and chamomile are the two most popular herbal teas on winter evenings in Türkiye. Both are made by steeping and are generally safe.

Key differences

  • Linden is accepted for traditional use in cold symptoms and mild mental stress; there are few clinical studies.
  • Chamomile is recognised for mild digestive complaints and mouth and throat inflammation; small studies on anxiety are positive.
  • Chamomile can cause allergy in people allergic to the daisy family; no important risk is known for linden.

Which one, when?

When you have caught a chill, linden; for an upset stomach, chamomile. Both should be steeped covered, not boiled.
