Peppermint vs lemon balm: what is the difference?

Peppermint and lemon balm belong to the same family (Lamiaceae); their leaves look alike, but their scents are very different: peppermint is cool and sharp, lemon balm smells of lemon.

Key differences

  • Peppermint has strong evidence: peppermint oil capsules reduced irritable bowel symptoms in many studies.
  • Lemon balm is used mostly for mild stress and sleep problems.
  • Peppermint can worsen reflux, and pure peppermint oil must not be applied to babies’ faces. Lemon balm needs care in people taking thyroid medication.

Which one, when?

For indigestion and gas, peppermint; for tension, lemon balm.
