Frankincense or myrrh? Two incense resins

Frankincense (Boswellia) and myrrh (Commiphora myrrha) are two incense resins of the same family, burned for thousands of years; they are separate genera with separate monographs.

Key differences

  • In Anatolia the name “günlük” is also given to the sweetgum tree and its resin; that is a different plant.
  • Frankincense is probably safe by mouth; in studies it has been used for up to 6 months.
  • Myrrh is used for mouth and gum inflammation; the tincture must not touch the eyes, and boils on the face should be treated by a doctor.
  • Indian myrrh (guggul) is a separate species; its studies do not apply to myrrh.

Which one, when?

As incense, both should be burned in a ventilated room, not a closed one.
