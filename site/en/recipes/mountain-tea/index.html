Mountain tea (Sideritis)

The winter tea of the Taurus highlands: a light, floral tea steeped with the flowering stems.

Prep: 2 minTotal: 12 minMakes: 2 cups

Ingredients

  • 1 handful of dried mountain tea (flowering stems)
  • 2 cups of just-boiled water
  • Optional: lemon and honey

Method

  1. Break the stems to fit your teapot.
  2. Pour the water over them.
  3. Steep for 5–10 minutes.
  4. Strain and drink, with lemon and honey if you like.

To protect endemic species that are over-harvested in the wild, choose mountain tea from cultivated plants.

Who should be careful? Generally considered safe as a tea. For lack of data it is not recommended in pregnancy, while breastfeeding or for young children. Over-harvesting threatens some endemic species, so tea from cultivated plants is the better choice.
