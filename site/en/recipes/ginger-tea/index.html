Ginger tea

A sharp, warming winter tea simmered with slices of fresh ginger.

Prep: 3 minTotal: 15 minMakes: 1 cup

Ingredients

  • 3–4 thin slices of fresh ginger (about 2 cm)
  • 1 cup of water
  • Optional: a few drops of lemon and 1 teaspoon of honey

Method

  1. Wash the ginger and slice it thinly, skin on.
  2. Put the slices and water in a small pot and bring to the boil.
  3. Turn the heat down and simmer gently for 10 minutes.
  4. Strain into a cup; once it has cooled a little, add lemon and honey if you like.

For a milder taste, grate the ginger instead and steep it in boiling water for 10 minutes rather than simmering it.

Who should be careful? Safe in food amounts. High doses can cause heartburn. People on blood thinners or with gallstones should ask a doctor before taking supplements.
