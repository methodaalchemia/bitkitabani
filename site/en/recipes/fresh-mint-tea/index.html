Fresh mint tea

A refreshing tea made in minutes with a handful of fresh mint.

Prep: 2 minTotal: 7 minMakes: 1 cup

Ingredients

  • 1 handful of fresh mint leaves (or 1 dessertspoon of dried mint)
  • 1 cup of just-boiled water
  • Optional: a slice of lemon

Method

  1. Wash the mint leaves and put them in a cup.
  2. Pour the water over them and cover the cup.
  3. Wait 5 minutes.
  4. Remove the leaves or strain, and drink.

In people with reflux, mint can make symptoms worse.

Who should be careful? Generally safe. Can worsen reflux. Pure peppermint oil must not be put on the faces of babies and young children; it can stop their breathing.
