Purple basil sherbet

A cooling sherbet from eastern Anatolia, made with purple basil, that turns pink with lemon.

Prep: 5 minTotal: 30 minMakes: 4 glasses

Ingredients

  • 1 large bunch of purple basil
  • 1 litre of water
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 4–5 tablespoons of sugar (to taste)

Method

  1. Boil the water and take it off the heat.
  2. Add the washed basil, stems and all; cover and steep for 15 minutes.
  3. Strain; while it is still hot, stir in the sugar until it dissolves.
  4. When cool, add the lemon juice; it will turn from purple to pink. Chill and serve.

The colour change from purple to pink comes from the anthocyanin pigment in basil reacting to the acid in lemon.

Who should be careful? Safe in food and tea. The essential oil and concentrated supplements contain estragole and should not be used in pregnancy, while breastfeeding or by children. The leaves are rich in vitamin K; people on warfarin should not suddenly eat large amounts.
