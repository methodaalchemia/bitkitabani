Thyme tea

An Anatolian winter tea with a sharp scent, made with a spoonful of dried thyme.

Prep: 1 minTotal: 11 minMakes: 1 cup

Ingredients

  • 1 dessertspoon of dried thyme
  • 1 cup of just-boiled water
  • Optional: 1 teaspoon of honey

Method

  1. Put the thyme in a cup or teapot.
  2. Pour the water over it and cover.
  3. Steep for 5–10 minutes.
  4. Strain and add honey once it has cooled a little.

What is sold as “kekik” in Türkiye can come from several species; the sharpest-smelling ones are rich in carvacrol.

Who should be careful? Safe in culinary amounts. Pure thyme oil is caustic; it should not be applied undiluted or swallowed.

About Thyme and oregano

Contains thymol and carvacrol, whose strong antimicrobial effect has been shown in the lab. The EMA recognises thyme (Thymus vulgaris) as a traditional herbal medicine for productive cough.
