Corn silk tea

A light tea of dried corn silk, known in Anatolia as a diuretic.

Prep
2 min
Total
12 min
Makes
1 cup

Ingredients

  • 1 dessertspoon of dried corn silk
  • 1 cup of just-boiled water

Method

  1. Cut the silk and put it in a cup.
  2. Pour the water over and cover.
  3. Steep for 10 minutes.
  4. Strain and drink; keep drinking plenty of water during the day.

Use silk from unsprayed, organic corn. Not for conditions that require limiting fluids, such as heart or kidney failure.

Who should be careful? Generally considered safe as a tea. Because it increases urine flow, avoid it where fluid intake must be limited, such as heart or kidney failure. High doses affected early pregnancy in rats, so avoid it in pregnancy. With signs of a urinary infection (fever, blood in urine, burning) see a doctor rather than relying on tea.
