Cardamom Turkish coffee

A fragrant coffee brewed with cardamom, loved in south-eastern Anatolia, Hatay and Arab cuisine.

Prep: 2 minTotal: 7 minMakes: 1 cup

Ingredients

  • 1 heaped teaspoon (about 7 g) of Turkish coffee
  • 1 coffee cup of cold water
  • 1–2 cardamom pods
  • Optional: sugar

Method

  1. Lightly crush the cardamom pods with the flat of a knife.
  2. Mix the coffee, water, cardamom and sugar if using in a cezve.
  3. Cook over low heat without stirring until it foams up.
  4. Spoon the foam into the cup, bring the rest up once more and pour.

Crushing the cardamom just before brewing, rather than using it pre-ground, keeps much more of its scent.

Who should be careful? Safe as a spice. The long-term safety of high-dose supplements is not well known; in pregnancy, stay with food amounts. It may cause allergy in people allergic to the ginger family.
