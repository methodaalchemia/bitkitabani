Nettle soup

A soft, spinach-like soup made with young spring nettle leaves.

Prep: 10 minTotal: 35 minMakes: 4 bowls

Ingredients

  • 2 handfuls of young nettle leaves (picked with gloves)
  • 1 onion
  • 2 tablespoons of olive oil
  • 2 tablespoons of rice or 1 tablespoon of flour
  • 5 cups of water or stock
  • Salt and chilli flakes

Method

  1. Wash the nettles wearing gloves and remove the tough stems.
  2. Fry the onion in the olive oil until soft; add the rice or flour and stir for a minute.
  3. Add the water and nettles; once boiling, simmer for 20 minutes.
  4. Blend if you like; season with salt and chilli flakes.

Nettles lose their sting completely when cooked. Pick only the young spring leaves, before the plant flowers.

Who should be careful? The fresh plant causes itching and welts. Because it is a diuretic, people taking blood pressure or diabetes medicines should be careful.
