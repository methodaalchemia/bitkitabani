Fennel tea

A sweetish, anise-scented tea made with lightly crushed seeds.

Prep: 2 minTotal: 12 minMakes: 1 cup

Ingredients

  • 1 teaspoon of fennel seeds
  • 1 cup of just-boiled water

Method

  1. Lightly crush the seeds in a mortar or with the flat of a knife.
  2. Put them in a cup and pour the water over them.
  3. Cover and steep for 10 minutes.
  4. Strain and drink.

Crushing the seeds lets the essential oil pass into the water; whole seeds make a much weaker tea.

Who should be careful? Because it contains estragole, the EMA advises against long-term use in young children and pregnancy. People allergic to celery or carrot may react.

About Fennel

The EMA recognises fennel seed for mild digestive complaints, gas and period pain. There are small studies on infant colic.
