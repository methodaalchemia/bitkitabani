In short

What is Autumn crocus?

Autumn crocus (Colchicum autumnale ve diğer türler) is a plant of the Colchicaceae family. Traditions that use it: Anatolia and the Mediterranean. Part used: corm and seeds (pharmaceutical industry only).

What is Autumn crocus used for?

Traditional use: Used in folk medicine for rheumatism and gout, which led to poisonings. Scientific evidence: clinical. Colchicine from this plant is still the main drug for gout and Familial Mediterranean Fever (FMF).

Is Autumn crocus safe?

Safety label: high risk. The whole plant is deadly poisonous. Poisonings happen when its leaves are mistaken for wild garlic or leeks, or its flowers for saffron. Symptoms start hours later.

Traditional use

Anatolia

Used in folk medicine for rheumatism and gout, which led to poisonings. Known for the pink-purple flowers that appear leafless in autumn meadows.

Ancient Anatolia

It takes its name from Colchis (the eastern Black Sea coast), famous for its poisonous herbs.

In nature

Where it grows
Damp meadows and mountain pastures; Türkiye has many species.
Harvest season
Not to be collected. Flowers appear September–November, leaves in spring.
Local names
güz çiğdemi, acı çiğdem, itboğan

Mix-up risk

Confused with the saffron crocus and edible spring crocuses (Crocus). An autumn crocus flower has 6 stamens; a Crocus has 3.

Preparation

Never prepare it as a home remedy.

What science says

Colchicine from this plant is still the main drug for gout and Familial Mediterranean Fever (FMF). Because the amount in the plant cannot be controlled, the plant itself is deadly.

Safety

High risk

The whole plant is deadly poisonous. Poisonings happen when its leaves are mistaken for wild garlic or leeks, or its flowers for saffron. Symptoms start hours later.

Drug interactions

Medicine or substanceWhy it matters
People taking colchicineThe plant contains the same poison; overdose symptoms multiply.
Macrolide antibiotics (clarithromycin), some antifungals, statinsSlow the breakdown of colchicine or increase the risk of muscle damage.

This list is not complete. If you take regular medication, ask your pharmacist or doctor before trying Autumn crocus.
