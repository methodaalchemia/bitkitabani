In short

What is San Pedro cactus?

San Pedro cactus (Echinopsis pachanoi) is a plant of the Cactaceae family. Traditions that use it: Andes. Part used: stem.

What is San Pedro cactus used for?

Traditional use: On Peru’s northern coast and in the highlands, healers serve huachuma in all-night “mesa” ceremonies to see the cause of illness and break sorcery. Scientific evidence: traditional. Contains mescaline.

Is San Pedro cactus safe?

Safety label: high risk. Causes severe nausea, vomiting and racing heart and carries psychiatric risks. Dangerous for people with heart disease or a history of psychosis. Legal status: Restricted in Türkiye.

Traditional use

Andes

On Peru’s northern coast and in the highlands, healers serve huachuma in all-night “mesa” ceremonies to see the cause of illness and break sorcery.

History

Stone carvings at Chavín de Huántar (1st millennium BC) show figures holding this cactus, pointing to a tradition of over 3,000 years.

In nature

Where it grows
Andean slopes at 2,000–3,000 m (Peru, Ecuador). Also widely grown as an ornamental.
Harvest season
Can be cut all year.
Local names
No Turkish folk name. Local names: San Pedro, huachuma, wachuma

Mix-up risk

Resembles many columnar cacti. Mescaline content varies greatly between species and between plants.

Preparation

The stem is sliced and boiled for hours.

What science says

Contains mescaline. Its clinical effects have been studied far less than those of psilocybin and LSD.

Safety

High risk

Causes severe nausea, vomiting and racing heart and carries psychiatric risks. Dangerous for people with heart disease or a history of psychosis.

Drug interactions

Medicine or substanceWhy it matters
Antidepressants and MAO inhibitorsMay change the effect of mescaline unpredictably.
Blood pressure and heart medicines, stimulantsRaises blood pressure and heart rate.

This list is not complete. If you take regular medication, ask your pharmacist or doctor before trying San Pedro cactus.

Legal status

Restricted in Türkiye

Its mescaline is a controlled substance. The cactus is sold as an ornamental; preparing it for consumption may be illegal.

Laws differ by country; check local rules before travelling with or buying any plant product.
