Herbs for constipation and what to watch for

Herbs used for constipation fall into two groups: fibre-rich plants that add bulk, and laxatives that stimulate the bowel. Fibre can be used safely for a long time; stimulant laxatives only briefly.

In short

For long-term use the best choice is fibre such as flaxseed. Senna works but should only be used for a few days. Drinking aloe latex is not recommended.

The plants, strongest evidence first

Flaxseed Linum usitatissimum

Evidence: ClinicalGenerally safe

The European Medicines Agency recognises flaxseed for well-established use in constipation. Always take it with plenty of water.

Flaxseed: safety, interactions and sources →

Senna Senna alexandrina

Evidence: ClinicalCaution

Accepted for short-term treatment of occasional constipation. Do not use it for more than a week; using it to lose weight is dangerous.

Senna: safety, interactions and sources →

Aloe vera Aloe vera

Evidence: LimitedCaution

The yellow latex of the leaf is a strong laxative; it causes diarrhoea, cramps and potassium loss, so drinking it is not recommended.

Aloe vera: safety, interactions and sources →

When to see a doctor: See a doctor for new, persistent constipation, blood in the stool or weight loss. Laxatives should not be used regularly.
