Herbs for memory: do they really work?

Herbs said to boost memory are very popular. But studies testing these claims have mostly been disappointing.

In short

A large study showed that ginkgo does not prevent dementia in older people. Studies of rosemary and gotu kola have not shown a clear effect on memory either.

The plants, strongest evidence first

Ginkgo Ginkgo biloba

Evidence: LimitedCaution

Its standardised extract is recognised in Europe for mild age-related forgetfulness. But a large US study showed that ginkgo does not prevent dementia in older people. It can increase bleeding risk.

Rosemary Salvia rosmarinus

Evidence: TraditionalGenerally safe

There are small studies on memory, but the results are not conclusive.

Mountain tea Sideritis türleri

Evidence: TraditionalGenerally safe

A few small studies on attention and cognitive function gave limited results.
