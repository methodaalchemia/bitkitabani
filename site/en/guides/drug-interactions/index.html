Herb–drug interactions: which plants can cause problems with your medicine?

Plants contain active substances just as medicines do. Some can strengthen your medicine’s effect, some weaken it, and some add to its side effects. Below, the interactions reported in the catalog are grouped by medicine.

This list is not complete. It covers only interactions reported in the catalog entries; a plant missing from the list is not proof that it is safe with your medicine. If you take medicines regularly, ask your pharmacist or doctor before starting any herbal product.

Take special care

St John’s wort is the best-known plant that weakens many medicines, including birth control pills, blood thinners and some heart and transplant drugs. Taken with antidepressants it can cause serotonin syndrome.
