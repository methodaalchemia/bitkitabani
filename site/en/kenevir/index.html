In short

What is Hemp?

Hemp (Cannabis sativa) is a plant of the Cannabaceae family. Traditions that use it: Asia and Siberia, Anatolia and the Mediterranean. Part used: fibre, seeds, flowers.

What is Hemp used for?

Traditional use: Herodotus describes the Scythians throwing hemp seeds on hot stones and bathing in the smoke; vessels full of hemp seeds were found in the Pazyryk burial mounds in the Altai. Scientific evidence: clinical. Contains cannabinoids such as THC and CBD.

Is Hemp safe?

Safety label: caution. THC products can raise the risk of psychosis in young people and are addictive. Seeds and fibre are safe. Legal status: Licensed in Türkiye.

Traditional use

Central Asia

Herodotus describes the Scythians throwing hemp seeds on hot stones and bathing in the smoke; vessels full of hemp seeds were found in the Pazyryk burial mounds in the Altai.

Anatolia

Its fibre is woven into rope, sacks and cloth; the seeds are roasted as a snack. Kastamonu, Samsun and Rize are known for hemp weaving.

In nature

Where it grows
Central Asian in origin; in Türkiye it may be grown under licence in 19 provinces.
Harvest season
Harvested at the start of flowering for fibre, in autumn for seed.
Local names
kenevir, kendir, kendir otu

Mix-up risk

Industrial hemp and high-THC varieties look alike; only a lab can tell them apart.

Preparation

For fibre, the stalks are retted in water; seeds are roasted or pressed for oil.

What science says

Contains cannabinoids such as THC and CBD. Cannabinoid drugs are approved for chemotherapy nausea and some forms of epilepsy. Cannabis use is linked to psychosis risk and addiction.

Safety

Caution

THC products can raise the risk of psychosis in young people and are addictive. Seeds and fibre are safe.

Drug interactions

Medicine or substanceWhy it matters
Sedatives, sleeping pills, alcoholDrowsiness and slowed reactions increase.
Blood thinners (warfarin)CBD and THC may raise drug levels.
Seizure medicines (clobazam, valproate)CBD changes drug levels and may raise liver enzymes.

This list is not complete. If you take regular medication, ask your pharmacist or doctor before trying Hemp.

Legal status

Licensed in Türkiye

Growing is allowed only in permitted provinces with Ministry of Agriculture and Forestry approval. THC-containing products count as narcotics.

Laws differ by country; check local rules before travelling with or buying any plant product.
