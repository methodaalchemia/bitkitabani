In short

What is European mistletoe?

European mistletoe (Viscum album) is a plant of the Santalaceae family. Traditions that use it: Anatolia and the Mediterranean, Europe. Part used: leafy twigs.

What is European mistletoe used for?

Traditional use: In folk use the twigs are brewed for blood pressure and blood sugar. Scientific evidence: limited. In Europe, mistletoe extracts are injected as complementary therapy for people with cancer.

Is European mistletoe safe?

Safety label: high risk. The berries and high doses are poisonous, especially to children. It can lower blood pressure. The TİTCK monograph writes that, for lack of sufficient data, it should not be used in pregnancy or while breastfeeding.

Traditional use

Anatolia

In folk use the twigs are brewed for blood pressure and blood sugar. Bird-lime was made from its sticky berries; its Turkish name comes from this.

Europe

The Roman writer Pliny describes Gaulish druids ceremonially cutting mistletoe from oaks with a golden sickle.

In nature

Where it grows
On apple, poplar and pine branches in the Black Sea and Marmara regions.
Harvest season
In winter it is easily seen and gathered on trees that have shed their leaves.
Local names
ökse otu, gökçe, burç, çekem

Mix-up risk

Mistletoe from different host trees can differ in content. Keep the berries away from children.

Preparation

Only standardised preparations are used, under a doctor’s supervision. ⚠️ The EMA has not adopted a monograph for this species and published a public statement instead; so there is no dose defined at EU level.

What science says

In Europe, mistletoe extracts are injected as complementary therapy for people with cancer. There are studies on quality of life, but it has not been shown to treat cancer.

Safety

High risk

The berries and high doses are poisonous, especially to children. It can lower blood pressure. The TİTCK monograph writes that, for lack of sufficient data, it should not be used in pregnancy or while breastfeeding.

Drug interactions

Blood pressure medicinesMay increase the effect by lowering blood pressure.
ImmunosuppressantsMay stimulate immunity.
Cancer treatmentMistletoe extracts are used as supportive cancer care in some countries; only with the oncologist’s knowledge.
